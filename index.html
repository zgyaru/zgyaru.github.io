About

I am a Research Associate at the University of Cambridge, working on AI-based approaches in ageing biology. My research focuses on ageing biology, single-cell and spatial omics, cancer genomics, tumour microenvironment, and AI-assisted biomedical discovery.

I received my Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Wenzhou Medical University, where I studied cancer genomics and single-cell analysis. My long-term research interest is to understand how ageing-related cellular states, tissue environments, and molecular programmes shape disease progression, tumour initiation, and therapeutic response.
