About

I am a Research Associate at the University of Cambridge, working on AI-based approaches in ageing biology. My research focuses on ageing, single-cell and spatial omics, cancer genomics, tumour microenvironment, and computational methods for biomedical discovery.

I received my Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Wenzhou Medical University, where I studied cancer genomics and single-cell analysis. Before that, I completed my bachelor's degree at Harbin Medical University, with research experience in deep learning and microRNA structure.

My long-term research interest is to understand how ageing-related cellular states, tissue environments, and molecular programmes shape disease progression, tumour initiation, and therapeutic response.

Research

Ageing Biology

I study how ageing reshapes tissue structure, cellular composition, immune infiltration, senescence programmes, and organ-specific molecular signatures.

Single-cell and Spatial Omics

I use single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, and multi-omics integration to investigate cellular heterogeneity, cell-state transitions, and tissue microenvironments.

Cancer Genomics and Tumour Microenvironment

My previous work focused on tumour microenvironment heterogeneity, exhausted T cell states, cancer-associated fibroblasts, cancer organoids, and immunotherapy-associated cellular states.

AI for Biomedical Discovery

I am interested in developing AI-assisted tools and knowledge systems for literature mining, biological reasoning, high-throughput data interpretation, and automatic research reporting.

Technical Skills

High-throughput data analysis, NGS data analysis, bulk RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, whole-genome sequencing, genome-wide association studies, artificial intelligence models, and programming in R, Python, and Bash.

Projects

Ageing Knowledge Base and Literature Agent

A growing knowledge system for collecting, organising, and interpreting ageing-related literature, gene sets, cell types, and biological mechanisms.

Keywords: ageing, literature mining, knowledge base, AI agent.

Spatial Transcriptomics of Mammary Gland Ageing

Spatial analysis of mammary gland tissue to investigate how ageing and genetic background reshape epithelial states, immune infiltration, senescence-associated programmes, and tissue microenvironments.

Keywords: Visium HD, spatial transcriptomics, mammary gland, ageing, senescence.

Single-cell Analysis of Tumour Microenvironment and Immunotherapy

Computational analysis of tumour-infiltrating immune cells, cancer-associated fibroblasts, pathway activity, and cellular interactions associated with immunotherapy response.

Keywords: single-cell RNA-seq, T cells, CAFs, immunotherapy, cancer.

AI-assisted Single-cell Analysis Agent

A prototype AI agent designed to support single-cell data analysis, biological interpretation, automatic figure generation, and research reporting.

Keywords: AI agent, single-cell analysis, automated reporting, computational biology.
